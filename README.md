# PARA Hub

A complete, lightning-fast, and bilingual PARA productivity app that lives entirely inside Obsidian. 

PARA Hub turns your Obsidian vault into a beautiful, app-like dashboard to manage your Projects, Areas, Resources, and Archives without ever leaving your notes. All your data is stored as plain Markdown files, meaning you own your data forever.

## ✨ Features

- **Complete PARA System**: Built-in support for Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives, and an Inbox.
- **Lightning Fast**: Uses a smart memory cache (v2.0) so the app stays instant, even if you have thousands of notes.
- **Premium Dashboard**: A beautiful, modern UI with quick-action toolbars, stat cards, and smooth progress bars.
- **Bilingual Interface**: Instantly switch between English and Formal German (Hochdeutsch) right from the settings.
- **Weekly Review**: A guided 9-step checklist to help you reflect and plan your week, with automatic progress saving.
- **Data Safety**: All notes are saved as standard Markdown with simple metadata. If you ever turn off the plugin, your notes still open and make sense.
- **No External Dependencies**: No Dataview, no external servers, no build steps. Just pure Obsidian.

## 🚀 How to Install

### Method 1: From the Obsidian Community Store (Coming Soon)
*Once approved by the Obsidian team, you will be able to install it directly:*
1. Open Obsidian and go to **Settings** -> **Community plugins**.
2. Click **Browse** and search for **PARA Hub**.
3. Click **Install**, then **Enable**.

### Method 2: Manual Installation
If you want to install it manually right now:
1. Download the latest release files (`main.js`, `manifest.json`, and `styles.css`).
2. Open your Obsidian vault folder and navigate to `.obsidian/plugins/`.
3. Create a new folder named `para-hub`.
4. Place the three files inside the `para-hub` folder.
5. Open Obsidian, go to **Settings** -> **Community plugins**, click **Reload plugins**, and turn on PARA Hub.

## 📖 How to Use

PARA Hub organizes your life into five buckets. You can access all of them from the navigation bar at the top of the app:

- 📁 **Projects**: Outcomes with a finish line (e.g., "Plan a wedding").
- 🗂️ **Areas**: Ongoing responsibilities you maintain (e.g., "Health", "Finances").
- 📚 **Resources**: Reference material you want to keep (e.g., "Recipes", "Meeting notes").
- 🗄️ **Archives**: Inactive things. We never delete anything; we archive it.
- 📥 **Inbox**: A temporary holding area for your quick thoughts.

**The Dashboard:**
When you open PARA Hub, you are greeted with your dashboard. Here you can click **Quick Capture** to save a thought to your Inbox, check your **Tasks due today**, or open your **Weekly Review**. 

**Editing & Archiving:**
To keep the interface clean, secondary actions (like Edit or Archive) are hidden behind a `...` (More Options) menu on every task and project card.
